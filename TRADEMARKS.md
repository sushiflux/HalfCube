## English

The [PolyForm Shield License](LICENSE) covers the code of HalfCube. It says nothing about the **name**, the **logo** and the **look** of the program. This file does. It tells you what you can do without asking, what needs permission, and why.

"HalfCube", the logo and the look belong to SushiFlux. At the moment the name is not registered as a trademark. The rules below apply anyway: they are the conditions under which you may use the name and the logo.

### What you can do without asking

- **Talk about HalfCube.** Write articles, reviews, tutorials, forum posts or videos. Use the name as it is: "HalfCube".
- **Show the program.** Use screenshots and screen recordings of HalfCube, unchanged and in their real state. Cropping to the part you talk about is fine.
- **Say what works with it.** You may write "for HalfCube", "works with HalfCube" or "compatible with HalfCube" for add-ons, scripts, models, templates, courses and files you made. Make clear that your work is yours and not an official part of HalfCube.
- **Link to the project.** A link with the name and the logo as a plain link or button is fine, as long as the logo is shown unchanged.
- **Use the program as it is.** Everything the license allows, such as selling parts you designed with HalfCube, needs no extra permission. You do not have to put the HalfCube logo on your parts or your work.
- **Change HalfCube for your own use.** The license allows that. A changed copy that you only use yourself or in your company may keep the program's own name, because nobody else sees it as a product.

### What needs permission

Ask first (open an [issue](https://github.com/sushiflux/HalfCube/issues) or write to the developer) before you:

- put the name or the logo in the **name of your own product, company, domain or social media account**, even with extra words ("HalfCube Pro", "HalfCube Plus", "Half-Cube");
- use the logo or a look-alike on **merchandise** (shirts, stickers, mugs) or in a **shop**;
- give a changed copy of HalfCube to **other people** with the original name or logo;
- say or suggest that SushiFlux **approves, supports or runs** your project;
- use the name or the logo in an **advertisement**.

Permission is given in writing and for the case you describe.

### Changed versions and forks

The license excludes offering a product that competes with HalfCube. The rules for name and logo add this:

- If you pass a changed version on to others, give it **its own name** and **its own logo**. They must not be confusable with HalfCube.
- State clearly that your version is a changed version and **not** the original. You may say that it is "based on HalfCube" and link to the project.
- Keep the notices that the license requires (file `NOTICE`).

### The logo

The logo is a cube whose left half is a sketch and whose right half is a solid body.

- **Never change it.** Do not redraw it, recolour it, stretch it, rotate it, crop it, add effects or take parts out of it. You may scale it up and down in the same proportions.
- Keep clear space around it. Do not put it on a busy background that makes it hard to see. The sketch half stays white, also on dark backgrounds.
- Do not combine it with another logo into a new mark.
- Take the logo from the project itself (folder `branding`), not from a redrawn copy.

### The wordmark

The wordmark is the name "HalfCube" in two tones, always written with a capital H and a capital C and without a space. The colours are fixed and do not change with a theme:

| Background | "Half" | "Cube" |
|---|---|---|
| Light | #16476E | #1F75AE |
| Dark | #FFFFFF | #4CD1E0 |

In running text write "HalfCube" in plain letters. Do not write "Half Cube", "halfcube" or "HALFCUBE" for the product name.

### The look of the program

The way HalfCube looks (its own icons, colour themes and design) is part of its identity. Do not build a product that imitates the look so closely that people take it for HalfCube. Taking inspiration for your own, different design is fine.

### If something goes wrong

Do not worry about honest mistakes. If you use name or logo in a way that does not fit these rules, you will get a friendly request to change it first. Only if that does not help, further steps may follow.

### Questions

Open an [issue](https://github.com/sushiflux/HalfCube/issues) and describe what you plan. Rules like these can be extended later; this file then gets a new date.

### Geänderte Fassungen und Forks

Die Lizenz nimmt das Anbieten eines Produkts aus, das mit HalfCube im Wettbewerb steht. Die Regeln für Name und Logo ergänzen:

- Gibst du eine geänderte Fassung an andere weiter, gib ihr **einen eigenen Namen** und **ein eigenes Logo**. Sie dürfen nicht mit HalfCube zu verwechseln sein.

### Das Logo

Das Logo ist ein Würfel, dessen linke Hälfte eine Skizze und dessen rechte Hälfte ein Volumenkörper ist.

- **Verändere es nie.** Zeichne es nicht neu, färbe es nicht um, strecke, drehe oder beschneide es nicht, setze keine Effekte darauf und nimm keine Teile heraus. Du darfst es im gleichen Seitenverhältnis vergrößern und verkleinern.
- Lass Freiraum um das Logo. Setze es nicht auf einen unruhigen Hintergrund, auf dem man es schlecht sieht. Die Skizzenhälfte bleibt auch auf dunklem Grund weiß.
- Kombiniere es nicht mit einem anderen Logo zu einer neuen Marke.
- Nimm das Logo aus dem Projekt selbst (Ordner `branding`), nicht aus einer nachgezeichneten Kopie.

### Die Wortmarke

Die Wortmarke ist der Name „HalfCube“ in zwei Farbtönen, immer mit großem H und großem C und ohne Leerzeichen. Die Farben sind fest und ändern sich nicht mit einem Farbthema:

| Hintergrund | „Half“ | „Cube“ |
|---|---|---|
| Hell | #16476E | #1F75AE |
| Dunkel | #FFFFFF | #4CD1E0 |

Im Fließtext schreibst du „HalfCube“ in normaler Schrift. Schreibe den Produktnamen nicht „Half Cube“, „halfcube“ oder „HALFCUBE“.

### Das Aussehen des Programms

Wie HalfCube aussieht (eigene Symbole, Farbthemen und Gestaltung), gehört zu seiner Identität. Baue kein Produkt, das das Aussehen so genau nachahmt, dass man es für HalfCube hält. Sich für ein eigenes, anderes Design inspirieren zu lassen, ist in Ordnung.

### Wenn etwas schiefläuft

Mache dir wegen ehrlicher Fehler keine Sorgen. Verwendest du Namen oder Logo auf eine Weise, die nicht zu diesen Regeln passt, bekommst du zuerst eine freundliche Bitte, es zu ändern. Nur wenn das nicht hilft, können weitere Schritte folgen.
